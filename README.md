# SmartSeat-AI
Intelligent-Hybrid-Workplace-Desk-Reservation-Platform

SmartSeat AI is a full-stack hybrid-workplace platform that combines time-based desk reservations, team bay allocation, WFO tracking, NFC/QR check-in, real-time occupancy, and AI-assisted workplace planning.

SmartSeat AI explores how modern web, real-time data, physical desk identification and AI can work together to solve hybrid-office seating problems.

The platform enables employees to reserve desks for specific time intervals, follow team-based WFO schedules, find alternative seating when they miss their regular office days and check into physical desks using QR/NFC.

Managers can manage team rosters and assigned seating areas, while workplace administrators can monitor real-time and predicted occupancy.

AI is used as an intelligence layer for natural-language search, recommendation and workplace planning, while booking permissions, access control and scheduling constraints remain deterministic application rules.
